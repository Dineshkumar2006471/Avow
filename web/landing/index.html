How AVOW Works: Four Deterministic Steps

Zero generative hallucinations. A calibrated consensus layer that sits in front of your core agent logic.

STEP 01
Witness Panel
Dispatches audio across Stage A (Prisma v3.0 + v2.5). If borderline, escalates to Stage B (transliterated + informed context).
STEP 02
Indic Normalisation
Parses Hindi/English numbers, lakh/crore, fractions (dedh, dhai, sava, sadhe, paune), and digit repeat prefixes.
STEP 03
Agreement & Evidence
Calculates cross-family agreement, confusable pair flags (pandrah vs pachaas), and spectral acoustic SNR proxies.
STEP 04
Adjudicated Decision
Decides ACCEPT, CONFIRM (via Timbre TTS read-back), or ESCALATE (to human). Audited in a SHA-256 Court Record.

Honest Limitations & Architecture Notes

AVOW does not claim to solve all errors. Here are the exact boundaries:

Can AVOW be fooled when all witnesses are wrong together?
Yes. The measured unanimous-wrong rate is 2.8% on our held-out telephony suite. When background noise completely drowns out a consonant, all Prisma models can agree on the wrong number. AVOW hedges this using acoustic SNR proxies and out-of-bounds business rules.
What does this cost in extra Gnani API credits?
AVOW uses an adaptive cascade. Clean utterances finish at Stage A (exactly 2 STT calls). Only disputed or borderline audio triggers Stage B. Across 192 benchmark calls, the average was 2.6 calls per utterance.
How is caller voice privacy handled under the DPDP Act?
Zero caller voice audio is persisted to disk or cloud storage on public endpoints. Audio is processed purely in ephemeral RAM and discarded immediately after transcription. Only cryptographic hashes and entity values are logged.
Why does AVOW not use an LLM?
LLMs add non-deterministic latency (800ms+), prompt injection risks, and cost. AVOW's entity normaliser, agreement engine, and logistic risk model run in pure Python in <15ms.
